I am a **Senior Data Scientist** at Aidentyx Inc. in San Jose, CA, with 10+ years of experience applying machine learning and advanced analytics to manufacturing and industrial systems.

My work focuses on **predictive maintenance**, **anomaly detection**, and **production AI systems**, including RAG and multi-agent architectures that turn large-scale engineering and operational data into actionable insights. I have led industrial analytics projects across semiconductor and discrete manufacturing domains to improve equipment reliability, process performance, and operational efficiency.

Previously I led the Data Science team at BISTelligence.ai and worked as a Data Scientist at BISTel in Seoul, collaborating with partners including Samsung Electronics, SK Hynix, LG Energy Solution, and Samsung Heavy Industries.

I received my **Ph.D. in Industrial & Management Engineering** from Korea University (advisor: Prof. Jun-Geol Baek), researching ensemble learning, data augmentation, and fault detection for industrial AI. I have also taught as an Adjunct Professor at Cyber University of Korea and as an instructor for applied data science programs.

### Research & applied interests
- Predictive maintenance (PdM) and prognostics & health management (PHM)
- Fault detection & classification (FDC) / statistical process control (SPC)
- Time-series anomaly detection and industrial sensor analytics
- Generative AI for engineering knowledge: RAG, LangChain/LangGraph, multi-agent systems
- Open-set recognition and equipment health indexing

### Current research
- **Industrial AI:** equipment health monitoring, predictive maintenance, anomaly detection, and decision support for manufacturing systems
- **Engineering knowledge systems:** RAG, multi-agent workflows, document-grounded assistants, and evaluation of LLM-based tools
- **Reliable AI evaluation:** LLM ensemble diversity and LLM-as-a-Judge reliability, reflected in current 2026 preprints

### Collaboration
I am interested in collaborations that connect rigorous machine learning research with real industrial data and deployment constraints. Potential areas include predictive maintenance and PHM, semiconductor and discrete-manufacturing analytics, engineering knowledge assistants, industrial RAG evaluation, and applied AI education. I welcome conversations with researchers, equipment and manufacturing companies, and teams looking to move from proof of concept to reliable production systems.
